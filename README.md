The datasets, codes and outputs support the study titled "Right species, wrong pond: environmental impact assessment of solar power development in Serbia overlooked the habitat of strictly protected crested newt (*Triturus ivanbureschi*)", which examines the role of solar energy infrastructure development on overlooked important reproductive centres of a nationally endangered amphibian in Serbia, and questions the potentially counter-productive effect of brief/baseline Environmental and socieal imact assessments. All analyses are performed in R and E-Surge and all datasets, outputs and code can be found within the corresponding folders.
